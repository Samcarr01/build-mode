Sheet 02 / How it works

Two AIs. One job each.

Claude is the planner. Claude Code is the builder. You pass messages between them, and that’s about it. Watch a build go round, or tap a step.

ClaudeThe planner
YouThe messenger
Claude CodeThe builder
Step 1 of 6

Claude · the planner

You say “I want to build a habit tracker”. It asks you a few questions, then picks the right tools and tells you why.

Two AIs check the work before you ever have to. You’re the last resort, not the first.

Sheet 03 / Twelve modes

Just say it.

No commands to learn. Type what you’d normally say, like “it’s broken” or “make it look better”, and it knows what to do. Go on, try it.

Waiting for you

Say something.

It reads what you said and picks one of twelve modes, from Kickoff to Undo.

Sheet 04 / Its notes

It keeps notes, so you don’t have to.

Everything it knows is saved as a few files inside your project. Leave it for a month and it picks up where you left off. Tap a file to see what it’s for.

The plan: every step in order, and what “finished” looks like.

Sheet 05 / Proof, not promises


Plain English. Safety built in.


Every update starts with three plain lines. Every save is checked for the mistakes that cause real trouble, without you having to ask.






After task M1-T3 · example


What changedThe habit list screen is in, and it matches the mockup.


Anything risky?Nothing risky.


What you need to doOpen the preview and tick a habit.


The detail sits underneath. Any technical word comes with a few plain words in brackets, like commit (a saved copy of the code).






Passwords left in the code?
Checked on every save, so they never end up online.


Add-ons with known security holes?
Checked before anything is saved.


A serious problem spotted by the reviewer?
Nothing gets saved until it’s fixed.


Could one customer see another customer’s data?
Tested every time. If they can, it can’t go live.
